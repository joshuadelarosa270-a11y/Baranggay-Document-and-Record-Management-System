Bridging the Digital Divide

The Hybrid Service Model

As discovered during field interviews in Barangay Wawa, not all residents have access to high-speed internet or smartphones. Our system supports both modalities:

  • Online Request Portal Residents with internet access can submit details ahead of time and avoid waiting in long lines.
  • In-Person Walk-in Encoding Barangay staff can immediately encode walk-in requests using the staff portal for elderly or non-tech-savvy residents.
  • Physical Verification & Dry Seal Final official documents are signed and stamped with the barangay dry seal upon personal appearance.
Data Privacy & Protection

Protecting Resident Information

Resident privacy is paramount. In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), our system implements enterprise-grade protection:

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Only authorized staff (Secretary, Punong Barangay) can view resident records and approve certifications.
  • Two-Factor Tracking Authentication Residents must provide both Reference Number AND Last Name to prevent unauthorized access to personal records.
  • Immutable Audit Logging Every verification, approval, certificate printing, and record access event is permanently logged with timestamps.
